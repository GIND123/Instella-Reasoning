# Audit — reliability-B120-K5-clean, and the plan for the next 15 GPU-hours

Date: 2026-07-26 · Auditor pass over `main` @ `526706d` · Target: NeurIPS 2026 workshop
(working deadline **2026-08-29 AoE** — ~34 days; confirm against the specific workshop CFP).

**Verdict: do not submit the current numbers.** Four of the five headline findings are
artifacts, not results. The engineering scaffolding is strong (honest atlas gating,
FDR correction, cluster bootstrap, resumable HF checkpointing) — the defects are in
measurement validity, and every one is fixable inside the 15-hour budget. The good news
is that fixing them opens a *better* study than the one currently planned.

### 1.2 F1 — the contamination contrast has no treatment group

`accuracy_gap.json` reports `gap: 0.0, z: 0.0, p_value: 1.0`. The reason is structural:

```
labels: Counter({'partial': 451})     # zero 'contaminated'
```

`compute_accuracy_gap` contrasts `contaminated` vs `clean`; `compute_stratified_accuracy_gap`
explicitly drops `partial` ([accuracy_gap.py:276](../src/instella_reasoning/analysis/accuracy_gap.py#L276)).
With no contaminated items the analysis is a no-op, and every stratum reports `contaminated n=0`.

This is not a threshold-tuning problem, it is a **design** problem:

- benchmark = GSM8K **test** ([loaders.py:229](../src/instella_reasoning/datasets/loaders.py#L229))
- corpus = `amd/Instella-GSM8K-synthetic` **train**, i.e. data *derived from GSM8K train*

GSM8K test items are not in a corpus built from GSM8K train. Zero contamination is the
**correct** answer, and no amount of scanning will produce a treatment group.

The 451 `partial` labels are false positives. They are driven purely by MiniLM cosine in
(0.75, 0.90] with **`char_ngram_jaccard = 0.0` and `word_ngram_overlap = 0.0`** — zero
lexical overlap. Top hit for `gsm8k_00000` (Janet's ducks, cosine 0.752) is a *different*
problem about Jane's chickens. That is topical similarity between two grade-school word
problems, which is what MiniLM is supposed to do. Reporting "451 contamination hits" in a
paper would be actively misleading.

### 1.3 F2 — the Math model was truncated, not regressed

This is the most consequential finding in the audit.

```
model                    emitted '####'    acc WITH marker    acc WITHOUT
Instella-3B-Math-hf       12/556 ( 2%)     0.583 (n=12)       0.217 (n=544)
AMD-OLMo-1B               10/556 ( 2%)     0.000 (n=10)       0.007 (n=546)
Instella-3B-Instruct     190/556 (34%)     0.695              0.626
Instella-3B (base)       299/556 (54%)     0.548              0.444
```

81% of Math outputs exceed 1700 chars (≈ the 512-token cap). Sampling the failures:

> `expected: 18 | extracted: '9'`
> `…Calculating that: Money made = 9 * $2 = $18  Wait, let me check again… 9 * 2 = $18.
>  That seems right. But let me verify each step again… So 9 eggs left. Each sold at`  ← cut off

**145 of 556 Math outputs (26%) contain the correct gold answer but are scored wrong.**
The checkpoint is a long-CoT self-verifying reasoner; it never reaches `####` inside 512
tokens, and the last-number fallback grabs a mid-computation value.

I re-scored the committed generations with a truncation-tolerant extractor: **Δ = +0.002.**
A better parser does not fix this — the answer is genuinely not in the truncated text. Only
a longer generation budget does. Math's true accuracy is plausibly 0.45–0.60, not 0.225,
which would **reverse the sign of the A6 finding.**

Note also the format-compliance spread (2% → 54%): the four models are currently being
compared on *instruction-following*, not reasoning.

### 1.4 F4 — three independent defects in the numeric variant generator

**(a) Structural constants are conflated with question leaves — wrong ground-truth labels.**

`gsm8k_00001`: *"A robe takes 2 bolts of blue fiber and half that much white fiber."*
Calc chain `<<2/2=1>>`, `<<2+1=3>>`. The generator extracts leaf `{2}` and substitutes it
everywhere in the chain:

```
'2/2' -> '5/5' = 1.0
'2+1' -> '5+1' = 6
generator's answer: 6
correct answer:     5 + 5/2 = 7.5   (and the item is now ill-posed)
```

The `2` meaning *"half"* is indistinguishable from the `2` meaning *"bolts of blue fiber"*
under value-based substitution ([gsm_symbolic.py:163-192](../src/instella_reasoning/gsm_symbolic.py#L163-L192)).

I hand-adjudicated all 6 at-risk parents: **4 confirmed mislabeled** (`gsm8k_00001`,
`00009`, `00027`, `00065`), 2 correct (`00082`, `00116`). That is **17/116 numeric rows
(15%) carrying a wrong gold answer.**

A related sub-bug: `_INT_TOKEN = re.compile(r"\d+")` splits decimals, so `10*1.2` yields
leaves `{10, 1, 2}`. In `gsm8k_00009` this rewrote *"overtime pay of 1.2 times"* into
*"overtime pay of 5.2 times"* and leaked an intermediate into the question text.

**(b) Systematic magnitude inflation — precisely the confound the 2026 critique names.**

Leaves are resampled from `[v//2, 2v+2]`, whose expectation is ≈1.25 v:

```
gold answer, median:   original 75.0  ->  variant 196.0   (2.61x)
variant answer LARGER than original in 99/116 = 85% of cases   (null = 50%)
```

[arXiv:2605.28700](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28700) shows a K-S shift of 0.12 in
GSM-Symbolic's integer distribution explained the significance of *half* the models they
re-analysed. This generator's shift is far larger. As it stands, the measured "reasoning
fragility" is substantially a **bigger-arithmetic** effect.

**(c) 22.5% template yield, so the headline rests on 22–26 items.**

```
  49  40.8%  leaf not exactly once in question
  27  22.5%  ACCEPTED
  18  15.0%  leaf collides with a step result
   9   7.5%  final answer != last step
   8   6.7%  duplicate step results
```

The conservative guards are good engineering but leave n=26 parents (n=22 after removing
the mislabeled ones). Nothing at that sample size will survive review.

**With a difficulty-matched comparison** (originals restricted to the 22 clean templated
parents), the corrected picture is:

| Model | original | numeric | drop |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Instella-3B-Instruct | 0.773 | 0.556 | −0.217 |
| Instella-3B (base) | 0.682 | 0.556 | −0.126 |
| Instella-3B-Math | 0.455 | 0.162 | −0.293 |

Base and Instruct **converge to identical accuracy (0.556) under perturbation** — the
instruction-tuning gain evaporates. That is a genuinely interesting claim, and it is worth
building the next run around. It is currently supported by 22 items and contaminated by
defects (a) and (b).

*Correction to a figure in my first pass:* comparing numeric variants against **all** 120
originals understates every drop, because the 26 templatable items are easier than average
(Instruct scores 0.731 on them vs 0.717 overall). The matched numbers above are the correct
ones.

### 1.5 What is solid

Genuinely good, keep it: the atlas `accuracy_only` / `insufficient_data` gating
([atlas.py:41-53](../src/instella_reasoning/analysis/atlas.py#L41-L53)) — it is rare and
commendable that the code refuses to emit a verdict it hasn't earned; BH-FDR correction;
parent-cluster bootstrap; excluding answer-changing variants from the consistency term
([METHODOLOGY.md §3](METHODOLOGY.md)); the loader bug-fixes; HF checkpoint/resume.

Why the test suite (123 passing) missed all of this: every `test_gsm_symbolic.py` test
asserts *structural* properties (leaves extracted, counts, `None` on malformed input).
**No test asserts that a generated variant's gold answer is semantically correct.**

## Part 2 — What the literature demands

| Source | Requirement it imposes |
|---|---|
| [Mirzadeh et al., GSM-Symbolic, ICLR 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05229) | Numeric perturbation is the decisive probe; templates are **hand-verified**. Auto-derived templates need explicit validation. |
| [*The Importance of Being Statistically Earnest*, arXiv:2605.28700](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28700) | **GLMM with per-question random effects**, not pooled proportions. Control the integer-magnitude confound explicitly. Only ~half of 20 models showed significant drops under correct analysis. |
| [Revisiting GSM-Symbolic (2026)](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Ze4C99Dasj74YKCFh/revisiting-gsm-symbolic-do-2026-frontier-models-still-fail) | GSM-NoOp-style "irrelevant" clauses are often *genuinely ambiguous*; the effect shrinks drastically after auditing them out. |
| [apple/GSM-Symbolic (HF)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/apple/GSM-Symbolic) + [apple/ml-gsm-symbolic](https://github.com/apple/ml-gsm-symbolic) | 100 official hand-written templates × 50 instances, plus P1/P2. **Using it removes the label-correctness threat entirely** and makes results comparable to published numbers. |
| [Instella tech report, arXiv:2511.10628](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10628) | Stage 2 explicitly targets GSM8K and includes Dolmino-Mix-1124, Tülu-3-SFT, and `Instella-GSM8K-synthetic`. GSM8K **train** is verifiably in the training data; **test** is not. |

On this repo's `irrelevant_context` variants: they insert genuinely null clauses
("This happened on a Tuesday afternoon", "A friend watched the whole time") drawn from only
~3 recycled templates. That side-steps the GSM-NoOp ambiguity critique — good — but it is a
weak probe a reviewer will read as a length-robustness check, not a distractor test.

## Part 3 — The redesign: use the one thing no closed model can offer

The current A1 asks *"are GSM8K test items in the training data?"* The answer is no, and
that question is unanswerable-by-construction on this corpus.

**Ask instead: does the model do better on problems it provably memorised?**

GSM8K **train** items are verbatim in Instella's Stage-2 data (WORKLOG §2.1 confirms doc #0
is verbatim GSM8K-train "Natalia sold clips…"). GSM8K **test** items are not. Both splits
are same-distribution, same-annotators, same-difficulty. That is a clean natural experiment
with a **verifiable** treatment assignment — exact 13-gram match against the corpus, not a
noisy cosine proxy.

The 2 × 2 and its predictions:

|  | original | numerically perturbed |
|---|---|---|
| **seen** (GSM8K train, verified in corpus) | A | B |
| **unseen** (GSM8K test, verified absent) | C | D |

- **Memorisation:** A ≫ C, and (A−B) ≫ (C−D) — the seen-item advantage collapses under perturbation.
- **Reasoning:** A ≈ C, and (A−B) ≈ (C−D).

`(A−C) − (B−D)` is a difference-in-differences estimate of the memorisation component, and it
**cancels the magnitude confound** — both arms get the same perturbation distribution. This
is the paper. It is only possible on a fully open model, which is exactly the pitch.

## Part 4 — The 15-GPU-hour plan

### 4.0 Throughput basis

Observed: 2,224 generations × 4 models at `max_new_tokens=512` ran in ~1.5 h on the
RTX PRO 6000 (96 GB) → ≈1,500 gen/h. Raising to `max_new_tokens=1024` (mandatory, see §1.3)
gives a conservative **≈1,000 gen/h → ~15,000 generations in 15 h.**

### 4.1 Blocking fixes before any GPU time (all CPU, ~1 day)

| # | Fix | Why |
|---|---|---|
| P0-1 | `max_new_tokens` 512 → **1024–1536**; add a per-model check that ≥90% of outputs terminate naturally | F2. Without this every number is a format-compliance measurement |
| P0-2 | Reject templates where a leaf's token count in the calc chain ≠ its count in the question | Kills the `2/2` conflation (§1.4a) |
| P0-3 | `_INT_TOKEN` → `\d+(?:\.\d+)?`; treat decimals as atomic | Kills the `1.2 → 5.2` bug |
| P0-4 | Resample leaves **magnitude-neutral** (e.g. multiplicative jitter centred on 1.0) and log the answer-magnitude ratio as a run assertion (target median ≈ 1.0×) | Kills the confound arXiv:2605.28700 names (§1.4b) |
| P0-5 | Add `apple/GSM-Symbolic` as a loader (`gsm_symbolic_official`) | Removes the label-correctness threat from the headline; gives comparability |
| P0-6 | Add tests that assert **semantic** correctness of ≥20 hand-checked variants | The gap that let all of this through |
| P0-7 | Hand-verify every surviving auto-derived template (expect 40–80 — a few hours) | GSM-Symbolic hand-annotated all 100; reviewers expect it |
| P0-8 | Use a **fresh `OUT` dir** for the new run | Score files are completion markers — a changed variant set in an old dir will be silently skipped (this is why `-clean` exists) |

### 4.2 Generation budget

| Block | Items | Models | Gens | Hours |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **B1** Seen-vs-unseen core: 250 GSM8K-train + 250 GSM8K-test, difficulty-matched, × (1 original + 3 surface + up to 3 numeric) | ~2,500 | 3 | 7,500 | ~7.5 |
| **B2** Official `apple/GSM-Symbolic`: 100 templates × 5 instances + 100 matched GSM8K originals | 600 | 3 | 1,800 | ~1.8 |
| **B3** Decoding-noise control: 100 items × 5 samples at T=0.7 | 500 | 3 | 1,500 | ~1.5 |
| **B4** Scale anchor: AMD-OLMo-1B on B1, **few-shot prompted** | 2,500 | 1 | 2,500 | ~1.3¹ |
| **Buffer** re-runs, failed cells, Math at 1536 tokens | — | — | ~1,700 | ~1.7 |
| | | | **~15,000** | **~13.8 h** |

¹ 1B model, roughly 2× throughput.

**B3 is not optional.** At temperature 0 the current run has zero decoding variance, so a
reviewer will ask whether the perturbation drop exceeds run-to-run noise. B3 is the answer.

**On AMD-OLMo-1B:** 408/556 outputs were degenerate loops. Zero-shot CoT on a 1B *base*
model does not work. Either prompt it 5-shot (standard for base models) or drop the A5
scale claim — the current comparison measures instruction-following, and
[METHODOLOGY.md §6](METHODOLOGY.md) already concedes the axis is confounded.

### 4.3 Analysis required (CPU, after generation)

1. **GLMM**, per arXiv:2605.28700: `correct ~ seen * perturbed * model + log(answer_magnitude) + (1 | item)`, logistic, via `statsmodels.genmod.bayes_mixed_glm.BinomialBayesMixedGLM`. This replaces the pooled two-proportion z-test as the headline test. Keep the existing cluster bootstrap as a robustness check.
2. **Difference-in-differences** `(A−C) − (B−D)` with a cluster bootstrap CI over parent items.
3. **Magnitude-matched sensitivity**: re-run the primary contrast restricted to variants whose gold answer is within ±20% of the original.
4. **Format-compliance covariate**: report `#### `-emission rate per model and confirm it no longer separates the models after P0-1.
5. Keep BH-FDR across all reported contrasts.

### 4.4 Resume mechanics (verified working)

`GOVINDFROM/Instella-Reasoning` (private dataset, 88 files) holds three runs; the token in
`.env` authenticates as `GOVINDFROM` with fine-grained write. `-clean` is the valid one
(`B120-K5` and `B15-K5` share identical 15-item variant files — `B120-K5` resumed from stale
prep, which is why `-clean` was created).

Two things to know before relaunching:

- **`generations/` were never uploaded** (`_DEFAULT_IGNORE` in [hf_sync.py:36](../experiments/hf_sync.py#L36)). Only `scores/` are recoverable, so nothing can be re-scored offline — every fix needs re-generation. Set `HF_INCLUDE_GENERATIONS=1` this time; the raw text is what lets you re-score without burning GPU hours again.
- The 1.45 GB `synthetic_corpus.jsonl` **is** cached on HF, so Stage 1 will not re-download or re-embed. That is ~free.
