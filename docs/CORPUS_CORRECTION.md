# Corpus correction: what containment was measured against, and what it invalidates

Origin: two review comments from J. Liu (AMD), 2026-08-19, on the Math-AI draft. Both are
correct. Both were verified independently against the released datasets before any text was
changed. Reproduce with `scripts/verify_stage2_corpus.py`; raw containment values are in
`outputs/corpus_verification/`.

## 1. The stage-2 corpus is `train_119K`, not `train`

`amd/Instella-GSM8K-synthetic` ships two splits. The dataset card states: *"The dataset
consists of two splits: 'train' and 'train_119K'. For Instella-3B model second stage
pre-training we used the 'train_119K' split, which is a subset of the larger 'train' split."*

| split | rows | used in stage-2 pre-training |
|---|---|---|
| `train` | 1,367,882 | no — generated pool |
| `train_119K` | 119,014 | **yes** |

The study indexed `train` (`split="train"` in `CORPUS_SOURCES`, ~1.45 GB in memory), i.e. a
superset 11.5x larger, 91.3% of which the model never saw.

**Effect on the treatment arm.** Containment of the 7,473 GSM8K train questions, same 13-gram
rule, same normalisation, same rolling hash:

| corpus | >= 0.80 | <= 0.10 | mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| `train` (as published) | 2,223 (29.75%) | 1,194 | 0.546 |
| `train_119K` (correct) | 1,130 (15.12%) | 1,867 | 0.403 |

The 29.75% reproduces the published 303/1000, which confirms the recomputation is comparable.
Of the 2,223 items in the published high-containment arm, only **50.8% remain high** against
the corpus the model actually consumed; 44.4% fall into the excluded middle band and 4.8% fall
to the low arm. Roughly half the treatment arm was assigned on text the model never trained on.

**Calibration is unaffected.** Test-split false positives are 0/1,319 at threshold 0.80 against
both corpora (Wilson 95% upper bound 0.29%). Table 1 survives and tightens slightly when the
full 1,319-item test split is used instead of a 1,000-item sample.

**A placebo arm falls out of the correction.** 107 GSM8K train items have containment >= 0.80
against the released pool and <= 0.10 against `train_119K`: generated by the identical
procedure from the identical seeds, never shown to the model. They hold constant whatever makes
an item attract high containment while setting actual exposure to zero. This control does not
exist without the split distinction, and no closed model could supply it.

## 2. Post-training checkpoints have no exposure contrast

`nvidia/OpenMathInstruct-2` is listed on the Instella-3B-Instruct card under **SFT →
Instella-3B-SFT**, and is inherited by Instruct, Math-SFT and Math. It is generated from the
GSM8K and MATH train sets, and its `gsm8k`-sourced rows are the GSM8K train problems verbatim.

Containment of GSM8K train against OpenMathInstruct-2 `train_1M` — the **smallest** released
split, 1M of 14M rows:

| | >= 0.80 | mean containment |
|---|---|---|
| GSM8K train (n=7,473) | 7,424 (**99.34%**) | 0.994 |
| GSM8K test (n=1,319) | 0 (0.00%) | 0.001 |

Because even the smallest split already contains 99.3% of GSM8K train verbatim, the conclusion
does not depend on which OMI-2 split was used.

**Consequence.** From SFT onward, *both* arms of a contrast defined on stage-2 containment are
exposed to GSM8K-derived text. The contrast is not attenuated, it is undefined: the estimator
compares two exposed groups. The near-zero gaps reported at SFT (-0.000) and Instruct (+0.031)
are what an unidentified design must produce, and are not evidence about recall.

The draft states the primary interpretation rests on Instella-3B, SFT and Instruct because
their truncation is below 4%. Truncation was the right filter but not the only one: **only
Instella-3B (stage 2) has both acceptable truncation and a defined exposure contrast.**

| checkpoint | GSM8K-derived corpora consumed | contrast defined | truncation |
|---|---|---|---|
| AMD OLMo 1B | none | n/a (control) | 97.8% |
| Instella-3B-Stage1 | none | n/a (control) | 93.7% |
| **Instella-3B** | Instella-GSM8K-synthetic `train_119K` | **yes** | 3.3% |
| Instella-3B-SFT | + OpenMathInstruct-2 | no | 3.5% |
| Instella-3B-Instruct | + OpenMathInstruct-2 | no | 0.2% |
| Instella-3B-Math | + OpenMathInstruct-2, DeepScaleR, ... | no | 44.4% |

## 2b. The stage-2 contrast collapses too — GSM8K train arrives via Tulu-3

`allenai/tulu-3-sft-mixture` is in the **stage-2 pre-training mixture** for Instella-3B
(Instella-3B-Instruct model card, "Pre-training Stage 2"). Containment of GSM8K train
against all 939,343 rows:

| | >= 0.999 | mean containment |
|---|---|---|
| GSM8K train (n=7,473) | 7,254 (**97.07%**) | 0.971 |
| GSM8K test (n=1,319) | 1 (0.08%) | 0.002 |

GSM8K train reaches stage 2 through at least five independent routes inside Tulu-3, none of
which is the Instella synthetic corpus (row counts are lower bounds; matching was capped at
the first 60 token positions per row):

| rows | Tulu-3 subset | note |
|---|---|---|
| 9,030 | `tulu_v3.9_open_math_2_gsm8k_50k` | OpenMathInstruct-2's GSM8K portion, re-packaged |
| 4,608 | `numinamath_tir_math_decontaminated` | a subset labelled *decontaminated* |
| 3,787 | `flan_v2_converted` | FLAN v2 has carried GSM8K since before the contamination literature |
| 2 | `tulu_v3.9_wildchat_100k` | organic: users pasted the problems into chat |
| 1 | `tulu_v3.9_aya_100k` | organic (the GSM8K train item 0 "Natalia clips" problem) |

Two consequences.

**No Instella checkpoint supports the contrast.** Every GSM8K train item entered Instella-3B at
stage 2 regardless of its Instella-GSM8K-synthetic containment. The high/low arms therefore
differ in *marginal synthetic dose*, not in exposure versus non-exposure. The published null is
what a marginal-dose contrast between two ~97%-exposed groups must produce. Stage1 is the only
genuinely unexposed checkpoint and it sits at floor accuracy with 93.7% truncation, so it
cannot carry the estimate either.

**The recall half of the paper is not salvageable on this family**, and would not be
salvageable by expanding the arms, matching harder, or re-running generation. The estimand
does not exist.

**But the measurement is the better result.** "Decontaminated" in practice means
*test*-decontaminated; train re-ingestion is invisible to it and is precisely what a
train-versus-test design depends on. And the WildChat/Aya rows show benchmark items entering
training corpora through organic human usage, which curation cannot fully close.

## 3. What changes in the paper

Unaffected: the GSM-Symbolic fragility result (Table 2) — it uses no containment labels.
The calibration table, the extraction-tier and truncation measurement results, and the
reliability decomposition also stand.

Requires re-running: arm assignment, the deletion-variant sample, and the recall estimates.

Changed claims:
- "verified corpus exposure" must mean the stage-2 corpus as consumed, and must be recomputed.
- The recall null narrows from three checkpoints to one, plus the placebo panel.
- Exposure becomes a property of *(item, checkpoint)* rather than of the item alone.

Recovered power: the corrected corpus leaves 1,130 high and 1,867 low parents available against
the 163-per-arm design actually used. Matching at ~500-900 parents per arm shrinks the interval
by roughly 1.8-2.3x, turning a weak null at Instella-3B into a tight upper bound.
